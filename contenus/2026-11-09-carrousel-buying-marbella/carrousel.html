Question 12/6
01

How will you use it?

  • Holiday home for your family
  • Rental investment, short or long term
  • Renovate and resell
  • A plot to build on, or a larger project

The use decides the area, the type of property and the rules that apply. Tourist letting, for example, requires a registration that must be checked property by property.

Question 23/6
02

Which area fits
your way of life?

From Cabopino to Casares, each area lives differently: beach or golf, a lively centre or a quiet hillside, all year or seasonal.

Ask about schools, daily services, travel time to the airport and what the street feels like in winter.

Visit at different times of day before you decide.

Question 34/6
03

What is your
total budget?

The price is not the full cost. In Andalusia, add the purchase tax:

  • Resale: 7% transfer tax (ITP)
  • New build: 10% VAT + 1.2% stamp duty (AJD)
  • Plus notary, land registry and legal fees
General Andalusian rates in force in 2026 (Agencia Tributaria de Andalucía; Spanish VAT Law 37/1992). Reduced rates exist for specific cases. Confirm for your purchase.

Question 45/6
04

How will you
finance it?

Cash or mortgage: decide early.

Spanish banks do lend to non-residents, but each bank sets its own loan-to-value limit, generally lower than for residents.

Ask for a pre-approval before you make an offer, and keep funds ready for the deposit, taxes and fees.

Sources: Banco de España, customer guide on mortgage loans; Law 5/2019 on real estate credit. Conditions vary by bank and profile.

Question 56/6
05

Have the legal checks
been done?

  • Your NIE, the foreigner's tax number
  • A recent nota simple from the Land Registry: owner, mortgages, charges
  • Licences for the house and any extension
  • Property tax and community fees up to date
  • An independent lawyer on your side
